Keep It Fed

Oil it monthly

Any food-safe mineral oil. Flood it, let it drink for 20 minutes, wipe dry. Thirsty boards crack; fed boards last decades.

Never the dishwasher

Quick rinse, towel dry, stand it on edge. Soaking and dishwashers are how good boards go to an early grave.

Sand & refresh yearly

A pass of 220-grit and a fresh coat of board butter erases a year of dinner parties. Ten minutes, tops.
