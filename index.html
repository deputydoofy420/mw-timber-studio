Our Rings

Matt started making cutting boards as Christmas gifts in a backyard shed with one bandsaw and a stubborn streak. Twelve years later the shed is bigger, the glue-ups are wilder, and the plaid quota is strictly enforced.

Every board is cut, glued, planed and oiled by hand. Food-safe finishes only, no shortcuts, no plastic — and every piece gets a final once-over before it leaves the bench.
